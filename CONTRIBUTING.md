## Contributing

Join a project through [MLCommons Get Involved](https://mlcommons.org/get-involved/).
Pull requests from non-members are welcome.

Complete the [subscription and CLA steps](https://mlcommons.org/get-involved/#join-a-working-group).
The agreements are on the [MLCommons policies page](https://mlcommons.org/policies/).

Make changes in your fork, then open a pull request. Ensure that the CLA check and other checks pass.
